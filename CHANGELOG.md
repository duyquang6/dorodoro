# Changelog

Released builds, newest first. The full announcement for each release is in the

## 2026.10.07 — 2026-10-07

First public build, for spruceOS and stock TrimUI firmware.
Full notes: **[RELEASE.md](RELEASE.md)**.

**Added**
- A countdown that keeps running in the background while a game plays
- Presets of 15, 25, 45, 60 and 90 minutes, and any length to 240 minutes
- Rumble, a bell and red blinking LEDs when the time is up, for two minutes
- A short nudge at 5 minutes and at 1 minute left
- SELECT + START stops the alarm, from inside a game too
- Wakes the handheld from sleep when the time is up
- A clock-only screen while counting, always on or dark after 30 seconds
